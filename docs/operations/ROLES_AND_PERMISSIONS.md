# CPVP.PRO roles and permissions

## Public roles

### Guest

Can view public pages, learning previews, rules, FAQ, public profiles and public TierList pages when available.

### Member

Authenticated through Discord OAuth.

Can use account settings, notifications and public community features allowed for the current rollout stage.

### Verified Player

Member with a safely linked and verified licensed Minecraft Java Edition account.

Can submit tier-test applications and participate in official Vanilla CPVP ranking flows.

## Testing roles

### Regular Tester

Requirements:

- minimum current tier: `LT3`;
- selected through recruitment among `LT3` players;
- approved by staff;
- trusted and consistent;
- mandatory two-factor authentication.

Responsibilities:

- conduct low-tier tests;
- estimate approximate tier from gameplay;
- issue low-tier results up to `LT3`;
- identify HT3 skip-test candidates;
- keep required match records.

Status values:

```text
active
inactive
suspended
```

A tester loses testing permissions if the current tier drops below `LT3` or if staff removes the role.

### Senior Tester

Requirements:

- already be a regular tester;
- receive `HT3` or higher;
- be selected separately by staff;
- remain trusted and consistent;
- mandatory two-factor authentication.

Not every HT3 player becomes a senior tester.

Responsibilities:

- conduct HT3 skip-tests;
- spectate high-tier matches;
- review recordings;
- issue verdicts;
- participate in disputes.

No separate Head Tester role is required initially.

## Moderation roles

### Moderator

Can process reports, hide content, issue warnings, apply forum restrictions and maintain community order.

### Staff

Can:

- add or remove testers;
- appoint senior testers;
- resolve tester disagreements;
- manage rules;
- review appeals;
- manage punishments;
- access audit logs;
- forcibly remove a Minecraft link during an investigation when necessary.

### Administrator

Full platform administration access.

## Public visibility

- tester list is public;
- tester status is public;
- tester match history is stored;
- complaints against testers are supported;
- tier history is public;
- punishment badges are public with details on hover.
