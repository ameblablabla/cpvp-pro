# CPVP.PRO forum rules

> Public draft. Forum is a later-stage feature.

## Communication

1. Keep messages relevant to the topic.
2. Avoid insults and provocations.
3. Do not create duplicate topics.
4. Do not revive old topics without useful new information.
5. Do not spam.

## Advertising

- Advertising is allowed only in explicitly permitted sections.
- Mass promotion and hidden advertising are not allowed.
- Links must be relevant to the discussion.

## Content

- Do not publish private information without permission.
- Do not claim other people's materials as your own.
- Do not publish suspicious links or files.

## Reactions

Reactions and likes are supported under forum posts.

## TierList discussions

- Use official review and appeal flows for disputes.
- Do not pressure testers or staff.

## Moderator actions

Moderators may edit, move, hide or close content, issue warnings, mute users and restrict forum access.

## Appeals

Forum moderation appeals follow [`APPEALS.md`](APPEALS.md).
