# CPVP.PRO fair play and mod rules

> Public draft. The complete allowed-mod list will be finalized later.

## Fair play

Official matches must be played honestly. The following are prohibited:

- account sharing in ranked matches;
- arranged results;
- intentional throwing;
- fake score reports;
- automation that changes normal PvP interactions;
- client or server modifications that create an unfair advantage;
- tools that reveal normally unavailable opponent information;
- suspicious latency manipulation.

## Allowed optimizer examples

The following optimizer examples are allowed:

- Marlow's Crystal Optimizer;
- Hero's Anchor Optimizer;
- Hero's Elytra Optimizer;
- Totem Counter.

These are not the complete allowed-mod list.

## Other generally acceptable modifications

Usually acceptable when they do not create an unreasonable advantage:

- cosmetic mods;
- performance improvements;
- HUD elements that show information about the local player;
- visual customization that does not expose hidden opponent information.

## Disallowed categories

Examples:

- macros;
- auto-clickers;
- auto totem;
- reach changes;
- movement changes;
- inventory-movement changes;
- ESP;
- radar;
- free-look that provides an unfair advantage;
- opponent health or armor tracking unavailable in normal gameplay;
- double-bind abuse;
- harmful connection manipulation.

## Evidence

Reports require concrete evidence. Staff may request additional materials during investigation.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity, consequences may include Restricted, mute, temporary ban or permanent project ban.
