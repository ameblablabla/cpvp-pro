# CPVP.PRO cheating policy

> Public draft. Exact punishment mapping remains `TBD` until final staff review.

## Prohibited behavior

Official matches must be played honestly. The following are prohibited:

- account sharing in ranked matches;
- arranged results;
- intentional throwing;
- fake score reports;
- assisting another player in cheating;
- withholding relevant evidence during an investigation;
- unfair client, server, command or plugin advantages;
- suspicious latency manipulation.

## Evidence

Reports require concrete evidence. Staff may request additional materials from players, testers or spectators.

## Consequences

Depending on severity and history, consequences may include:

- Restricted status;
- mute;
- testing restriction;
- temporary account ban;
- permanent project ban.

Restricted escalation is defined in [`../operations/MODERATION_POLICY.md`](../operations/MODERATION_POLICY.md).

## Appeals

Appeals follow [`APPEALS.md`](APPEALS.md).
