By using CPVP.PRO, users agree to follow platform rules, fair-play rules, moderation decisions and applicable laws.

- Users are responsible for activity performed through their linked profile.

Paid learning subscriptions may unlock premium materials, profile cosmetics, early access and Discord roles. They must not provide competitive gameplay or tier-test advantages.
