# CPVP.PRO mod and client rules

> Public draft. The full allowlist will be finalized later.

## Allowed optimizer examples

The following optimizer examples are allowed:

- Marlow's Crystal Optimizer;
- Hero's Anchor Optimizer;
- Hero's Elytra Optimizer;
- Totem Counter.

These are examples, not the complete allowlist.

## Generally acceptable modifications

Usually acceptable when they do not create an unreasonable advantage:

- cosmetic mods;
- performance improvements;
- HUD elements that show information about the local player;
- visual customization that does not expose hidden opponent information.

## Prohibited categories

- macros;
- auto-clickers;
- auto totem;
- reach changes;
- movement changes;
- inventory-movement changes;
- ESP;
- radar;
- free-look that creates an unfair advantage;
- opponent health or armor tracking unavailable in normal gameplay;
- double-bind abuse;
- harmful connection manipulation.

## Responsibility

Players use mods at their own risk. A modification that changes how the client communicates with the server compared with normal gameplay may lead to investigation and sanctions.

## Reports

Reports require concrete evidence. Staff may request additional materials.
