# CPVP.PRO platform rules

> Public draft. RU and EN versions will be published in the product interface.

## Purpose

CPVP.PRO is a Crystal PvP community platform. Use the platform respectfully and do not interfere with other users, moderation, testing or platform stability.

## General rules

1. Do not impersonate another player, tester, moderator or staff member.
2. Do not publish knowingly false information.
3. Do not spam, flood or abuse platform features.
4. Do not publish malicious links, files or illegal content.
5. Do not attempt to bypass restrictions, cooldowns or moderation decisions.
6. Do not harass users or pressure testers and staff.
7. Use only the Minecraft account linked to your CPVP.PRO profile for official tests.
8. Follow the separate Vanilla CPVP testing, fair-play and mod rules.

## Profiles

- Public profile name follows the current Minecraft username.
- Minecraft username history is public.
- Discord contact is visible only to authenticated users.
- Tier history is public.
- Punishment badges are public; details appear on hover.
- Retired, Inactive and Restricted states are displayed clearly.

## Appeals

Appeals are handled under [`APPEALS.md`](APPEALS.md).

## Enforcement

Depending on severity, moderation actions may include:

- warning;
- mute;
- content hiding;
- forum restriction;
- testing restriction;
- Restricted status;
- temporary account ban;
- permanent project ban.
