# CPVP.PRO appeals

> Public draft.

## General rule

A user may submit one appeal for one punishment or one tier verdict.

## Deadline

Appeal deadline:

```text
7 days
```

## Required information

An appeal should include:

- the decision being appealed;
- reason for disagreement;
- relevant evidence;
- requested outcome.

## Review

- Appeals are reviewed separately from the original decision.
- Staff may request additional information.
- Final result and short reason are stored in the CPVP.PRO database.
- Internal notes may remain in Discord.

## Scope

Appeals may be used for:

- Restricted decisions;
- tier verdicts;
- tester complaints;
- forum moderation;
- account restrictions.

Exceptional second appeals are not enabled by default.
