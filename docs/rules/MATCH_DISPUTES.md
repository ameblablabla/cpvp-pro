# CPVP.PRO match disputes

> Public draft. Detailed edge cases may be expanded during implementation.

## Technical problems

A match may be replayed when a technical issue prevents a fair result.

Examples:

- connection failure;
- server-side problem;
- crash;
- major lag spike;
- broken match setup.

The tester responsible for the match decides whether a replay is required.

## Evidence

Staff may request:

- full recordings;
- a second participant recording;
- server details;
- ping information;
- spectator notes;
- additional context.

## High-tier disputes

For recorded high-tier matches:

1. Two testers review the full recording.
2. If both agree, the verdict is accepted.
3. If they disagree, staff resolves the case.
4. The player receives the final result and a short reason.
5. The final verdict is stored in the CPVP.PRO database.

## Appeals

A player may appeal a match-related decision under [`APPEALS.md`](APPEALS.md).
