# CPVP.PRO moderation and community requirements snapshot

## Tester roles

### Regular tester

Confirmed:

- minimum rank: `LT3`;
- recruitment is conducted among `LT3` players;
- added by staff;
- trusted by the project team;
- may lose the role for poor decisions or rule violations;
- cannot continue testing if own tier drops below `LT3`;
- public tester list required;
- tester statuses required: `active`, `inactive`, `suspended`;
- tester match history required;
- complaints against testers required.

### Senior tester

Confirmed:

- must already be a regular tester;
- must receive `HT3` or higher;
- selected separately by staff;
- not every `HT3` player becomes a senior tester;
- no separate Head Tester role is required;
- may lose the role for poor decisions or violations.

## Moderation

Separate public documents are required:

- `CHEATING_POLICY.md`;
- `MOD_CLIENT_RULES.md`;
- `MATCH_DISPUTES.md`;
- `APPEALS.md`.

Confirmed moderation principles:

- unfair play, account misuse and prohibited client advantages must be sanctioned;
- punishment depends on severity;
- available actions include Restricted, mute and account ban;
- punishment history should be public;
- appeals are allowed;
- one appeal is allowed by default.

Detailed sanctions matrix and mod list remain `TBD` and should be adapted from the external reference.

## External channels

Confirmed:

- Discord is the main external community channel;
- Telegram is required as an announcement channel;
- on-site news are probably not required initially;
- on-site direct messages are not required because Discord is sufficient.
