# CPVP.PRO FAQ

## What is CPVP.PRO?

CPVP.PRO is a bilingual Russian-English Crystal PvP community platform. It is not limited to tier testing. The project includes learning, profiles, rules, notifications, Find Duel, teams and later Vanilla CPVP TierList features.

## What launches first?

The first public product milestone is the learning section.

## What competitive category is planned first?

```text
Vanilla CPVP
```

It is one overall rating category. Detailed kit rules will be added later.

## Which Minecraft version is supported?

Minecraft Java Edition on PC or laptop.

The mobile site is for convenient viewing, learning, profiles, settings, notifications and later applications.

## How does sign-in work?

Site sign-in uses Discord OAuth. Discord account is required.

## How will Minecraft linking work?

Tier-test applications require a linked licensed Minecraft Java Edition account. The preferred approach is official Microsoft OAuth. CPVP.PRO must never request or store passwords or session tokens.

## What is the tier ladder?

```text
LT5 < HT5 < LT4 < HT4 < LT3 < HT3 < LT2 < HT2 < LT1 < HT1
```

## How do low-tier tests work?

A regular tester evaluates actual gameplay in a `First to 4` match and may assign any low-tier result from `LT5` to `LT3`.

## How does the HT3 skip-test work?

A `4:0` or `4:1` win against a regular tester grants `LT3` immediately and gives the player an optional bonus test against a senior tester. Winning upgrades the result to `HT3`.

## How are high-tier matches checked?

Use either:

1. a full uncut recording;
2. a senior tester spectating the full match.

Recorded matches are reviewed by two testers. If they disagree, staff resolves the case.

## How often can a normal test be requested?

Once per week. Cooldown starts after the verdict.

## Are there paid subscriptions?

Yes. Paid learning subscriptions will have multiple levels and may include premium learning materials, top-level profile cosmetics, early access and Discord roles.

## Will Discord and Telegram exist?

Yes. Discord is the main community channel. Telegram is used for announcements.

## Will the site have a forum?

Yes, but forum is intentionally a later-stage feature.
