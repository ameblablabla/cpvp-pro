# CPVP.PRO community and subscriptions

## External channels

### Discord

Main community channel. Discord server should be bilingual.

Suggested sections:

```text
INFORMATION
├── announcements
├── rules
├── roadmap
└── faq

COMMUNITY
├── general
├── crystal-pvp
├── find-duel
├── training
└── feedback

SUPPORT
├── help
├── reports
└── appeals

TEAM
├── moderators
├── testers
└── development
```

### Telegram

Telegram is used as an announcement channel.

## Find Duel

Required fields:

- region;
- tier;
- server;
- ping.

Optional fields may be added later.

## Teams

First-version team model:

- name;
- tag;
- logo;
- description;
- leader;
- members;
- join applications;
- public team page;
- team rating;
- team matches.

## Following players

Player following should support configurable notifications.

Possible events:

- tier changes;
- important official matches;
- public profile updates;
- team activity.

## Reactions

Required:

- reactions under guides;
- reactions under forum posts.

## Paid learning subscriptions

Use multiple paid subscription tiers.

Possible benefits:

- premium learning materials;
- top-level profile cosmetics comparable to HT1 customization;
- early access to new features;
- dedicated Discord roles.

Subscription cosmetics and rank cosmetics should remain visually distinguishable.

## Important principle

Paid subscriptions must not provide competitive advantages in tier tests or gameplay.
